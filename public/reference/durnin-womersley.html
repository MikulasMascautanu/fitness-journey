Durnin–Womersley 4-site skinfold tables

Add the biceps, triceps, subscapular, and suprailiac readings in millimetres. Use the table for the sex in your profile and the age band containing your age on the measurement date.

These numerical values are transcribed from Durnin & Womersley (1974), British Journal of Nutrition 32, 77–97, Table 9, p. 95. View the original table (PDF, page 19).

Male table · Female table

The youngest published lookup bands are 17–29 for males and 16–29 for females, followed by 30–39, 40–49, and 50+. These are the Table 9 bands; the paper’s equation coefficients in Table 5 use different young age groups. Fitness Journey accepts adult profiles aged 18–120 and uses the published 50+ column for all ages 50 and above. The original sample included men up to 72 and women up to 68.

For a total between two rows, the app uses linear interpolation of their percentages and displays one decimal place. For example, a male aged 30–39 with 52.5 mm total is halfway between 21.5% at 50 mm and 22.5% at 55 mm: 22.0%. This interpolation is an app rule. Exact rows use the published percentage directly.

A dash means the source has no value. The app gives no estimate outside the available range for the selected column; it does not extrapolate. These are body fat estimates, and measurement technique affects results.
